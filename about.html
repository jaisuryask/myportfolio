About

Engineering ideas into real-world solutions

I'm Jai Surya S K, a pre-final year ECE student in Coimbatore who builds things where hardware and software meet.

I'm an Electronics and Communication Engineering student at Kumaraguru College of Technology. My interests are Embedded Systems, Robotics, IoT and Web Development, and I like projects that need all of them at once: a drone that samples water, a rocket that reports its own flight, a robot that follows a line.

I lead the Robotics and Automation Club as President and work on avionics with Kumaraguru SEDS, including CubeSat and Tenacity. I'm interested in building real-world engineering solutions and in continuously learning new technologies.

Outside the lab I enjoy inspiring others, leading with empathy and helping people grow, which is why club work matters to me as much as the circuits do.
